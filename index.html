Fair questions

What happens after the first 12 months?

Nothing changes, except you're free to go whenever you like. It rolls on month to month at the same price and you can cancel with 30 days' notice. If the price ever goes up, we'll tell you at least 60 days ahead.

What counts as a change?

Anything to do with what's already on your site: updating text, prices, opening hours, phone numbers, swapping photos, adding a staff member, a new service to your list, a holiday closure notice. Most clients send a few a month and there's no cap.

Bigger jobs, like a whole new section, an online shop or a full redesign, we'll quote up front before we start. You'll never get a surprise bill.

How fast are changes done?

Within one business day of your email or text, and usually the same day. We reply to let you know when it's live.

What if I want to leave?

After your first 12 months, give us 30 days' notice and you're done. Your domain and content are yours to take with you, and if you want the website itself, we'll transfer that to you too.

If you leave inside the first 12 months, the remaining months of the minimum are still payable. That's how we can build your site for nothing up front.

Do I need to buy hosting or a domain?

No. Hosting and your domain are both included. If you already own a domain, we'll use it. If you don't, we'll register one in your business's name. You'll need an ABN for a .com.au address.

Do I have to learn any software?

No. There's no login, no dashboard and no app. You email or text us like you would anyone else, and we do the rest.

Can I see examples?

Yes. Mention your trade or industry when you book a chat and we'll send you sites we've built for businesses like yours.

What if I already have a website?

We'll build you a new one and move your domain across with no downtime. Your email stays exactly where it is.
